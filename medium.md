| Warning Type | Details | Action Required |
|--------------|---------|-----------------|
| **Medication Interactions** | Metformin, Diuretics, Antacids deplete B vitamins | You NEED these supplements even more. Don't stop meds - supplement smarter |
| **B6 Toxicity** | High-dose pyridoxine HCL (>50mg) causes nerve damage | NEVER exceed 50mg. Use P5P form only. Check your multivitamin |
| **Wrong B1 Form** | Water-soluble B1 (thiamine mononitrate) = only 5% absorbed | Must use fat-soluble Benfotiamine. Regular B1 won't work |
| **Wrong ALA Form** | 50/50 synthetic blend blocks natural form | Only buy "Stabilized R-Alpha Lipoic Acid" |
| **Chemotherapy** | Antioxidants may interfere with chemo drugs | Consult oncologist before starting ALA or B vitamins |
| **Kidney Disease** | Kidneys process these minerals | Run all supplements by nephrologist first |
